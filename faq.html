Customer care

Good to know.

Helpful answers to the questions you may have before or after placing an order.

Orders & payments

Everything around placing an order.

How do I place an order?
Browse the shop, select a product and add it to your bag. Review your items, then follow the checkout steps. This current version is a front-end demo and does not send orders to a live store.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Payment options must be confirmed by the store owner. The demo checkout displays sample options only and does not process payments. Add your actual payment provider and supported methods before launching.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Contact customer care as soon as possible with your order reference. Changes may not be possible after an order has been packed or dispatched. Confirm the final policy with the store before publishing.
Where can I find my order reference?
In this demo, the reference appears on the order confirmation page after checkout. In a live store, it should also appear in your confirmation email or customer account.

Delivery & returns

Planning for your order to arrive.

How long does delivery take?
Delivery timelines depend on your location, courier and fulfilment arrangements. Publish verified delivery estimates for each service area before accepting live orders.
How much does delivery cost?
The prototype currently shows a sample ₦2,500 delivery charge for orders below ₦50,000 and free delivery at or above ₦50,000. Change these values to your real delivery rules before launch.
Can I return or exchange an item?
Please read the Shipping & Returns page. Return windows, eligible items, condition requirements and refund timing should be confirmed by the business before publication.

Products & care

Choosing pieces with confidence.

How do I choose the right size?
Check the product details and any size guide available. If you are unsure, contact customer care with the item name and your usual fit preference. Add a measured size chart for your actual products.
Will an item be restocked?
Availability can change. Contact the team about a specific piece or subscribe to store updates for announcements, if that service is enabled.
How can I reach the team?
Use the Contact page to send an enquiry. In this demo, the form does not send data to a server, so connect it to a real inbox or customer-support service before launch.
