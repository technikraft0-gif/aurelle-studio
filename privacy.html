Legal & transparency

Privacy policy.

A clear explanation of how a live Aurelle Studio store should handle personal information.

Publication note: This is a starter policy template, not legal advice. The store owner must adapt it to the actual business, services, processors and applicable privacy laws before publishing.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the services enabled, Aurelle Studio may collect information you provide when placing an order, contacting customer care, subscribing to updates or creating an account. This may include your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, order details and communication preferences.

2. How information may be used

Information may be used to fulfil orders, arrange delivery, respond to enquiries, provide order updates, manage returns, maintain store security and improve the customer experience. Marketing messages should only be sent where permitted and with any consent required by law.

3. Payments

Payment information should be handled by an appropriately selected payment service provider. Do not store full payment card details in ordinary website storage or send sensitive payment information through contact forms.

4. Cookies and local storage

This prototype uses browser local storage for demo cart items, wishlist selections and sample orders. These entries are stored on the current device and are not automatically synchronised with the store or another device. A production site may use additional cookies or similar technologies and should explain them accurately.

5. Sharing with service providers

A live store may need to share limited information with delivery partners, payment processors, hosting providers, customer-support services and other vendors. The business should document the providers it actually uses and ensure suitable safeguards are in place.

6. Retention and security

Personal information should be retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described, subject to applicable legal requirements. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be used, although no online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or restrictions on the use of personal information. You may also be able to withdraw consent for certain processing or unsubscribe from marketing messages.

8. Children

The store is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorisation where required by law.

9. Changes to this policy

This notice may be updated when store operations or legal requirements change. The live policy should display its effective date and explain how material changes are communicated.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the Contact page. Before launch, replace this instruction with the store's verified privacy contact details.
