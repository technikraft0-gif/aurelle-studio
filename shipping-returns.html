Delivery & care

Shipping & returns.

What to expect when your order leaves the studio, and what to do if something isn't quite right.

Before publishing: The details below are a starting point. Confirm actual delivery areas, courier rates, timelines, return windows and refund procedures before accepting real orders.

Delivery information

Orders should be dispatched to the address provided at checkout. Please ensure your delivery details and contact number are accurate. If a courier needs additional information, the store may contact you using the details you supplied.

Delivery fees

This prototype demonstrates free delivery on orders of ₦50,000 or more and a sample ₦2,500 fee for smaller orders. These are sample settings only. Confirm the final fees and any regional surcharges before launch.

Delivery timelines

Delivery estimates depend on the destination, dispatch schedule and courier. Add verified delivery windows for each service area and clarify whether the estimates refer to business days or calendar days.

Tracking an order

Use the Track an Order page to look up demo orders created in this browser. Real tracking requires order records on a server and a courier or fulfilment integration.

Returns and exchanges

If an item is unsuitable or arrives with a problem, contact customer care with your order reference and a description of the issue. The team can then advise on the next steps under the store's actual return policy.

Suggested return-policy details to confirm

  • The number of days customers have to request a return.
  • Whether items must be unworn, unused and returned with original tags and packaging.
  • Which items, if any, cannot be returned for hygiene or other lawful reasons.
  • Who pays return-delivery costs and how returned items are assessed.
  • Whether exchanges are offered and how replacement stock is reserved.

Refunds

Once a return is approved, the store should explain the refund method and expected processing period. The final policy must also describe how delivery charges, failed deliveries and defective or incorrect items are handled, in accordance with applicable consumer law.

Damaged, incorrect or missing items

Please contact the store promptly if an order arrives damaged, contains the wrong item or appears incomplete. Include the order reference and a clear description of the issue. If photographs are needed, the store should request only information relevant to resolving the enquiry.

Need help?

Visit our FAQs or contact customer care. For a demo order, you can also use the order tracking page.
